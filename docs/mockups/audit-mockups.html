Domo — Audit view, design explorations

Three takes on the Audit tab with a modern, shadcn-style visual language (neutral palette, soft badges, subtle borders, system type). Events are grouped into one activity per operation. Adapts to light/dark automatically. Mockups only — not wired to real data.

1

Master–detail table

Closest to today's app: a dense, sortable table of activities on the left with color-coded status badges; the full breakdown of the selected activity (meta, approved capability bounds, and a vertical timeline of every underlying event) on the right.

Activity feed

A calmer, more human "what happened" feed — one card per operation with an icon, a plain-language status badge, the goal it served, and an inline step trail. Better for glancing at recent activity than for forensic density. Cards can expand for the full timeline + raw events.
